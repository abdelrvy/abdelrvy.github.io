Blades and weapons

Blades use left click (combo) and right click (special attack). They gain mastery as you hit with them, and every Blade point adds damage.

Base damage, before mastery and Blade points. The Three Blades have no single special-attack number: it is three waves.

Novice Katana

Polished steel katana. Left click: 3-hit combo. Right click: Lightning Iai, a slashing dash. +0.5 damage per Blade point.

Novice Cutlass

Gold-guarded boarding cutlass. Left click: 4-hit combo. Right click: Boarding Rush, a slashing dash that leaves golden gashes. Same total combo power as the Novice Katana.

Tide Katana

Legendary blade from the depths. Left click: 3-hit combo. Right click: Tide Fang, a slashing wave that passes through enemies. +0.5 damage per Blade point.

Mâchefer's Axe

Kade Mâchefer's axe, torn from the tyrant of the Citadel. Left click: 3 heavy hit combo. Right click: Mâchefer Cleave, a wave that splits the ground. +0.5 damage per Blade point.

The Three Blades

Three katanas: one in each hand, the third between the teeth. Left click: 4-hit spinning combo. Right click: Blade Storm, three slashing waves that fly far ahead. +0.5 damage per Blade point.

Guardian's Khopesh

The golden sickle-sword of the Pyramid Guardian. Left click: 4-hit combo (slash, backhand, hook that pulls the enemy in, pharaoh's strike). Right click: Judgement of the Sun, three golden waves that fly far ahead. +0.5 damage per Blade point.
