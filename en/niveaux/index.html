Levels and Renown

Renown is earned by defeating enemies and finishing missions. Each level gives 3 points to spend on your stats. The maximum level is 250.

Renaissance

From level 150 you can do a Renaissance: you go back to level 1 and earn a star and 5 wheel tokens. Each star gives +10% Renown and gold (up to 5 stars) and +10% damage against enemies and bosses (up to 3 stars, never against players).

Hunting contracts open at level 100.
