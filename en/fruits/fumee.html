Smoke Fruit

The Smoke Fruit is a common fruit. Once eaten, it grants 4 attacks, unlocked as you master the fruit: the first at level 1, the last at level 75.

How to get it

  • Fruit wheel: 32.5% chance per spin. A rare-or-better fruit is guaranteed every 10 spins without one, an epic-or-better fruit every 35 spins.
  • Wild fruit: 30% of the fruits that grow on the islands.
  • Fruit merchant: always in stock, 5,000 gold.
  • Shop: permanent version for 100 V-Bucks.
