Rubber Fruit

The Rubber Fruit is a epic fruit. Once eaten, it grants 6 attacks, unlocked as you master the fruit: the first at level 1, the last at level 95.

How to get it

  • Fruit wheel: 5% chance per spin. A rare-or-better fruit is guaranteed every 10 spins without one, an epic-or-better fruit every 35 spins.
  • Wild fruit: 8% of the fruits that grow on the islands.
  • Fruit merchant: 250,000 gold, only when it is the fruit currently in stock. The merchant offers one new fruit at a time, drawn at random: this one comes up 3 times out of 14 (never twice in a row).
  • Boss loot: 1 chance in 20 of an epic fruit on each boss win with full loot, then this fruit among the 4 epic fruits.
  • Shop: permanent version for 500 V-Bucks.
