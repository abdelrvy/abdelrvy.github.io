Tide Colosseum

A ruined colosseum open to the sea, with its sand ring surrounded by water. Two gladiator camps to defeat; this island has no boss.

Overview

The Tide Colosseum is for players from level 235 to level 245. You fight Tide Netfighters and Bronze Champions there.

Boss

No boss on this island.

How to get there

By boat: get one at the shipyard on Trade Winds Island, then follow the mission arrow. On each island, the Harbor Master can make their dock your respawn point.
