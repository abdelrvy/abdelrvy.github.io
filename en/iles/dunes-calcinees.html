Overview

The Scorched Dunes is for players from level 90 to level 120. You fight Dune Raiders and Sirocco Sentinels there. Its boss: Pyramid Guardian.

How to get there

By boat: get one at the shipyard on Trade Winds Island, then follow the mission arrow. On each island, the Harbor Master can make their dock your respawn point.
