A Marine fortress taken over by mutineers. Grand staircase, terrace, courtyard and an arena at the top, where Kade Mâchefer waits.

Overview

The Marine Citadel is for players from level 30 to level 50. You fight Mutineers and Mutinous Gunners there. Its boss: Kade Mâchefer.

How to get there

By boat: get one at the shipyard on Trade Winds Island, then follow the mission arrow. On each island, the Harbor Master can make their dock your respawn point.
