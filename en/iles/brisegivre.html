Frostbreak

A frozen island, at night, under the snow. A village warmed by its fire, Trappers in the valley, an ice quarry, and Commodore Verglas at the top, on the Frozen Lake.

Overview

Frostbreak is for players from level 185 to level 230. You fight Frost Trappers and Icebreakers there. Its boss: Commodore Verglas.

How to get there

By boat: get one at the shipyard on Trade Winds Island, then follow the mission arrow. On each island, the Harbor Master can make their dock your respawn point.
