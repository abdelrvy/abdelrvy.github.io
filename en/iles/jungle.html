Jungle

A thick jungle around an ancient sanctuary. Monkeys and Gorillas roam it, and the Monkey King guards the arena at the top of the stairs.

Overview

The Jungle is for players from level 60 to level 80. You fight Agile Monkeys and Armored Gorillas there. Its boss: Monkey King.

How to get there

By boat: get one at the shipyard on Trade Winds Island, then follow the mission arrow. On each island, the Harbor Master can make their dock your respawn point.
