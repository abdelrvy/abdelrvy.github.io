Gold and shop

Gold is earned by playing: enemies, missions, bosses, chests, events and daily rewards. It is spent at merchants (fruits, blades, aura). V-Bucks are only for shop offers.

Wheel tokens

A wheel token gives one fruit draw. From level 5, a free token comes back every 2 hours. Bosses, level milestones, daily rewards and sometimes enemies give tokens too. Tokens can be traded between players from level 10.
