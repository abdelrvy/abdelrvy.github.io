Bandit Leader

Bandit Leader is the boss of Trade Winds Island. The quest opens at level 15.

Contents
  1. Mission
  2. Rewards

Mission

Talk to the quest giver to start the boss quest. During the quest, an arrow guides you to the arena.

Rewards

Renown1,450
Gold1,000
Bounty earned15,000

Anti-carry rule: a player below level 15 can help beat this boss, but their Renown, gold and mastery are capped and they get no rare loot (guaranteed tokens, fruit). From level 15 on, the reward is complete.
