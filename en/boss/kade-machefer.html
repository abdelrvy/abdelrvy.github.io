Kade Mâchefer

Kade Mâchefer is the boss of the Marine Citadel (the arena at the top of the Citadel). The quest opens at level 50.

Contents
  1. Mission
  2. Rewards

Mission

« Mâchefer took my fortress. Have you got what it takes to win it back? »— Commander Brisevent

Talk to the quest giver to start the boss quest. During the quest, an arrow guides you to the arena.

Rewards

Renown2,800
Gold2,500
Bounty earned35,000
Wheel tokens1

Anti-carry rule: a player below level 40 can help beat this boss, but their Renown, gold and mastery are capped and they get no rare loot (guaranteed tokens, fruit). From level 40 on, the reward is complete.
