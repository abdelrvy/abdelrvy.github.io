Events and chests

Treasure chests are hidden on the islands and come back after a delay. They have no name in the game: you tell them apart by the colour of their bands. Every few minutes, an event hits the whole server.

25-minute chest

Stay 25 minutes in the game to open this chest: it gives 200 gold per level (at least 5,000), a wheel token and, for 15 minutes, +50% gold and +50% mastery.
