Getting started

Your first steps in FruitBorne, in order.

  1. You arrive on Trade Winds Island. Talk to quest givers and defeat your first enemies to earn Renown and gold.
  2. Your first spin of the fruit wheel is free: it gives you a fruit and its power. After that, you need wheel tokens.
  3. Each level gives points: spread them across Health, Fists, Blade, Fruit and Weapon (see Levels and Renown).
  4. The first stage of the Aura is given on arrival: it raises your damage.
  5. Open treasure chests, stay 25 minutes for the time chest and come back every day for daily rewards (see Events and chests and Gold and shop).
  6. When your level is high enough, set sail: a boat takes you to the next island.
  7. Use a gift code for a head start.
