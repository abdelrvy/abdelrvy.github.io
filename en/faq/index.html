FAQ

How do I get a fruit?
From the fruit wheel (with a token), at the merchant, by finding a wild fruit, from some bosses or in the shop. Each fruit's page in Fruits and powers says how to get it.
Can I have two powers?
No, one power at a time.
What is the max level?
Level 250. See Levels and Renown.
What is a Renaissance?
From level 150 you restart at level 1 for permanent bonuses. See Renaissance.
Why does a boss give me few rewards?
Below a certain level, boss rewards are limited: it is an anti-carry rule. The level is on each page in Bosses.
